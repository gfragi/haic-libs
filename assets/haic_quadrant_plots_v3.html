Metric Formulas & Directions
EL — Effort Loss (X-axis)
EL = (t_actual − t_baseline) / t_baseline
Range: [0, ∞) · 0 = optimal · EL cannot be negative
← lower = better overhead
Tr — Trust Proxy (Y-axis)
Tr = accepted_suggestions / total_suggestions
Range: [0, 1] · behavioral proxy only
↑ higher = more acceptance

✓ Ideal (low EL, high Tr)

⚠ Automation Bias (low EL, low Tr)

Efficient but low acceptance. Humans may be ignoring AI. Check for skill degradation.

Metric Formulas & Directions
HCL — Human-Centeredness (X-axis)
HCL = 1 − (RT / RT_max)
Range: [0, 1] · RT = human reaction time
↑ higher HCL = LOWER cognitive load
F — Interaction Frequency (Y-axis)
F = N_interactions / (T_session / 60)
Range: [0, 10+] events/min · context-dependent
↕ neutral — optimal depends on task
Adjust Values
HCL (0=high load, 1=low load) 0.84
F (events/min) 3.6
Real Pilot Values
RAG Chatbot HCL=0.835, F=3.61±0.55
Quadrant Interpretation

—

Move the sliders to see interpretation.

⚠ HCL is inverted.
HCL = 1 means the human responded instantly (zero reaction time → zero load).
HCL = 0 means the human took maximum time (RT_max → full load).

✓ Ideal (high HCL, moderate F)

Humans respond fluidly. Interaction rate fits the task pace naturally.

✗ Overload (low HCL, high F)

Humans are slow AND overwhelmed with interactions. Immediate redesign needed.

⚠ Disengaged (high HCL, very low F)

Humans are fast but barely interacting. Possible automation bias or UI barriers.

Metric Formulas & Directions
A — Adaptability (X-axis)
A = tanh((Acc_late − Acc_early) / Acc_early)
Range: [−1, +1] · first/last 20% windows
Acc = acceptance rate in each window
↑ positive = humans improving trust over time
Tr — Trust Proxy (Y-axis)
Tr = accepted / total suggestions
Range: [0, 1]
↑ higher = more acceptance
Adjust Values
A (Adaptability, −1 to +1) +0.30
Tr (Trust Proxy) 0.67
Note: A uses 20% windows (first 20% and last 20% of session events), not the first and second halves. A positive A means the AI is becoming more trusted as the session progresses.
Quadrant Interpretation

—

Move the sliders to see interpretation.

✓ Growing Trust (positive A, high Tr)

Humans increasingly accept AI. Trust is building across the session.

⚠ Eroding Trust (negative A, moderate Tr)

Initial acceptance declining. Humans may be losing confidence in the AI.

✗ Abandonment (negative A, low Tr)

Low overall trust that is also declining. AI is not delivering value.

⚠ Ceiling Effect (near-zero A, high Tr)

Already at high trust — no room to grow. Ensure it's not automation bias.

Metric Formulas & Directions
⚗ Simulation Context Only
S — Surrogate Similarity (X-axis)
S = 1 − JSD(p_surrogate ∥ p_human)
Range: [0, 1] · JSD = Jensen-Shannon Divergence
1 = surrogate perfectly matches human distribution
↑ higher = surrogate more faithful
A — Adaptability (Y-axis)
A = tanh((Acc_late − Acc_early) / Acc_early)
Range: [−1, +1] · first/last 20% windows
↑ positive = trust improving
Adjust Values
S (Surrogate Similarity) 0.72
A (Adaptability, −1 to +1) +0.30
Note: S is only meaningful when a surrogate (synthetic) agent is used instead of real human participants. It quantifies how faithfully the surrogate replicates real human decision distributions. Do not compute S in real-user studies.
Quadrant Interpretation

—

Move the sliders to see interpretation.

✓ Valid Simulation (high S, positive A)

Surrogate faithfully replicates humans AND the AI improves over time. Results are trustworthy.

✗ Unreliable Simulation (low S, any A)

Surrogate doesn't match real humans. Adaptability results cannot be trusted.

⚠ Good Sim, Declining Trust (high S, negative A)

Simulation is valid but trust is eroding. Investigate AI quality or task framing.

— High S, Flat A (high S, A ≈ 0)

Valid simulation with stable trust. Normal for short or easy sessions.
